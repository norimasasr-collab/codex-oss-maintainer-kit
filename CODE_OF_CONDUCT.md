# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Giving specific and constructive feedback
- Respecting different experience levels and communication styles
- Accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes
- Protecting private or sensitive information

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
